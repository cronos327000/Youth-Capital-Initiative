ABOUT US

Empowering Future Financial Leaders

Youth Capital Initiative is dedicated to bridging the gap between traditional education and real-world financial wisdom.

Our Mission

To empower the next generation of financially informed and economically aware individuals by providing accessible financial education, practical investment knowledge, and real-world learning opportunities through workshops, competitions, and collaborative programs.

Our Vision

To build a global community of young learners who possess the knowledge, critical thinking skills, and confidence to make informed financial decisions, contribute to economic progress, and become responsible future leaders in an evolving economy.

THE COMPETITION BRIEF

Theme: Building an Investment Portfolio for the Future

Participants will construct a hypothetical investment portfolio and develop a clear investment strategy based on their market outlook, risk tolerance, and financial objectives.

The objective is not to maximize short-term returns, but to demonstrate sound investment reasoning, strategic thinking, effective risk management, and portfolio sustainability.

Virtual Capital Allocation Construct and manage a hypothetical portfolio across permitted publicly traded assets.
Evidence & Justification Defend asset choices using research, market analysis, and clear reasoning.

Judging Criteria

1. Understanding of the Problem

Understanding of core financial concepts and market context.

2. Quality of Research

Evidence-based research and credible data sources.

3. Creativity & Innovation

Innovative problem-solving and unique portfolio insights.

4. Feasibility of Solution

Practical realism, diversification, and long-term risk control.

5. Overall Presentation

Clarity, structure, and communication of investment decisions.

ASKED & ANSWERED

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Find instant answers to eligibility, fees, dates, and guidelines.

What is the registration fee for the competition?

The registration fee is $5 USD per entry. This covers individual participation (1 student) or a team entry (2 to 4 students).

Where do I submit my registration?

Registrations for India are OPEN currently via the official Google Form: Register Here.

International participants: International registrations will open in a few days. Follow @youthcapitalinitiativessr for updates.

Who is eligible to participate in the Challenge?

The competition is open to students currently enrolled in High School (9th to 12th grade) from any country.

Can I compete individually or as part of a team?

You may participate individually (1 student) or in a team of 2 to 4 students. Each participant may only be part of one team.

What modules are covered in the Financial Literacy Workshop?

The workshop syllabus covers 7 key modules: Why Investing Matters, Asset Classes, Risk vs Return, Building a Portfolio, Researching Investments, Common Mistakes, and the Competition Brief.

What asset classes are permitted in the competition?

Participants may construct portfolios using Stocks, Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs), Bonds, Commodities, Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), and Cash reserves.
